## SKY GUARD (every Sky still and clip)
ANATOMY LOCK for the whole image/clip: Sky has EXACTLY TWO long ribbon ears (one on each side of her head) plus ONE smooth indigo crest loop on the top-back of her head (part of her body, not hair), nothing else: no extra ribbons, no streamers, no scarves, no trails or tails coming off her head. The two ears are smooth single ribbons, indigo fading to pink tips; they hang down or flow gently beside/behind her; they never split, multiply, stand up, point upward or look like rabbit ears, horns or antennae. Her mitten hands are ALWAYS deep indigo-violet with three soft rounded digits (never white, never furry, never gloves). White only on her face, chest and belly. White crescent moon on the forehead. She always floats with a clear gap of air below her feet; she never stands, sits, walks or touches the ground or water. Movement is calm and slow (gentle drifting/gliding speed, never fast flying or falling). Exactly one character. No text, no watermark, no music.
(Never write the word "white" next to "hand", "mitten" or "glove" anywhere in the prompt.)
HEAD LOCK (Stanley, 23 Oct: hair kept coming out wrong): Sky has NO HAIR at all. No ponytail, no bun, no topknot, no pigtails, no hair strands or fringe, no hair tie, no hair band, no headband, no bow, no ribbon bow, no scrunchie, no clip, no crown, no accessory of any kind on her head. Her head is one smooth, round, glossy indigo shape (white only on the face mask), with the white crescent moon on the forehead. On the top-back of her head there is exactly ONE crest loop: a single thick, smooth, glossy indigo arch that grows out of the crown, leans backward and curves down to join the back of her head again, like a closed rounded handle or a folded petal, the same indigo material as her head with a soft starry sheen; it is part of her body, not hair, not tied, with no band, knot or tail where it joins and no loose end hanging from it. The two long ribbon ears grow from the sides of the head, below and in front of the crest; nothing else grows from the head.

CREST/CRYSTAL/PROPS LOCK (24 Oct): The crest arch sweeps BACKWARD and slightly to one side; from the front we see its curved upper edge rising behind the head (never an upright ring with a hole facing the camera). Her small diamond chest crystal stays pale blue (default form). Avoid back views of Sky in clips (the crest turns into a hood/ponytail and the moon pokes out like a horn): use three-quarter side views. Name every small prop that must stay (e.g. 'the little two-leaf sprout stays in the soil, unchanged, the whole clip'). Form changes: their own first+last-frame clip, prompt 'colour changes slowly in place like ink spreading; ears keep their exact shape and keep hanging down; EXACTLY two ears at every moment; nothing else grows on her head; no swirl, no burst'.

## ELEMENTAL FORMS
For an elemental form, the ear/tail material and shape follow that form's reference sheet (e.g. Ding Fire candle-flame ears), but the count rule still holds: EXACTLY TWO ears and one tail/plume, nothing extra; hands keep the default colour unless the sheet shows otherwise. The HEAD LOCK still applies in every form: no hair, ponytail, bun, bow, band or accessory; Sky's crest loop becomes only the form's own crest/leaf tuft as drawn on the sheet. The old tail is REPLACED by the elemental tail/plume (never two tails). The forehead symbol (Sky's white crescent moon, Season's golden sun) must stay visible in every form: write "keep the glowing white crescent moon on her forehead exactly as on the reference" in every form-change prompt and check it on every form still. Any change of form inside one clip (into a form or back to default) is a first+last frame video whose end still is made and QC'd first.

The Flow agent rewrites prompts. After each generation, read the item's prompt text in the media list; if it changed a guard (e.g. wrote "white mitten-like hand"), treat the output as suspect, QC extra carefully, and say "use my wording exactly" in the next request.
