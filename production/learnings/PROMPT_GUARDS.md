# Prompt guards — paste into EVERY Flow image and video prompt (living file)

Read `LEARNINGS.md` first. When a new mistake is found, add a learning there AND update the guard text here in the same run. The production task copies these blocks word for word.

## SKY GUARD (every Sky still and clip)
ANATOMY LOCK for the whole image/clip: Sky has EXACTLY TWO long ribbon ears (one on each side of her head) plus ONE small rounded loop tuft on top of her head, nothing else: no extra ribbons, no streamers, no scarves, no trails or tails coming off her head. The two ears are smooth single ribbons, indigo fading to pink tips; they hang down or flow gently beside/behind her; they never split, multiply, stand up, point upward or look like rabbit ears, horns or antennae. Her mitten hands are ALWAYS deep indigo-violet with three soft rounded digits (never white, never furry, never gloves). White only on her face, chest and belly. White crescent moon on the forehead. She always floats with a clear gap of air below her feet; she never stands, sits, walks or touches the ground or water. Movement is calm and slow (gentle drifting/gliding speed, never fast flying or falling). Exactly one character. No text, no watermark, no music.
(Never write the word "white" next to "hand", "mitten" or "glove" anywhere in the prompt.)

## SEASON GUARD (every Season still and clip)
ANATOMY LOCK for the whole image/clip: Season has EXACTLY TWO broad fanned ears (white with sky-blue tips, tan inside) and ONE large white-and-blue plume tail, golden eight-ray sun on the forehead, blue streak on his left flank, blue chest crystal. Two legs, always upright (never on all fours, never crawling, never animal-style flying); three fingers and a thumb on each hand; three toes on each foot. When he flies or floats he stays upright with legs hanging below him. Exactly one character. No text, no watermark, no music.
His two fanned ears stay fixed in their fanned shape on his head: they are NOT wings, never flap, never flop or hang down. He never crouches or leans onto his hands; hands never touch the ground. When he flies he floats calmly and slowly (no fast leaps or whip pans). Face stays sharp.
(Extend with Season learnings as they happen. Also: type every Flow request as ONE paragraph — Enter sends the message.)

## ELEMENTAL FORMS
For an elemental form, the ear/tail material and shape follow that form's reference sheet (e.g. Ding Fire candle-flame ears), but the count rule still holds: EXACTLY TWO ears and one tail/plume, nothing extra; hands keep the default colour unless the sheet shows otherwise. The old tail is REPLACED by the elemental tail/plume (never two tails). The forehead symbol (Sky's white crescent moon, Season's golden sun) must stay visible in every form: write "keep the glowing white crescent moon on her forehead exactly as on the reference" in every form-change prompt and check it on every form still. Any change of form inside one clip (into a form or back to default) is a first+last frame video whose end still is made and QC'd first.

## PROPS
- Water drops stay ONE clear water droplet, never ice, crystal or gems (unless the plan says crystal).

## CHECK THE AGENT'S REWRITE
The Flow agent rewrites prompts. After each generation, read the item's prompt text in the media list; if it changed a guard (e.g. wrote "white mitten-like hand"), treat the output as suspect, QC extra carefully, and say "use my wording exactly" in the next request.
