# The Ten Mirrors — 100 reels: every form in every world (V1, 7 Nov 2026)

Stanley's concept (7 Nov): show the personality of the 10 elements by putting each form into each of the 10 stem worlds. The pairing gives one of the Ten Gods, and each Ten God is a FEELING the viewer can name without knowing BaZi. Rotate Sky / Season and rotate forms and worlds so no two neighbouring reels look alike. One sample reel (TM001) is made and approved before anything is scheduled or published.

Follows Earth Discovery (same characters, same 3D Flow style, same end card V2, no words on screen, no episode numbers). Series chapter 5 in the Season One plan: "The Ten Mirrors".

## 1. The engine
- Forms: Sky = Gui, Yi, Ding, Ji, Xin (Yin). Season = Ren, Jia, Bing, Wu, Geng (Yang). The form is fixed for the whole reel (no change of form inside a reel — avoids every form-change failure in LEARNINGS L13–L41).
- Worlds (already built in Earth Discovery; reuse those backgrounds as the look): Jia the giant-tree forest (SEA03), Yi the sprout garden (SKY03), Bing the sunlit plains (SEA04), Ding the candlelit night garden (SKY04), Wu the mountain pass (SEA05), Ji the farm field (SKY05), Geng the iron cave (SEA06), Xin the crystal field (SKY06), Ren the open ocean (SEA02), Gui the rain valley (SKY02).
- Feeling = Ten God of the world stem seen from the form (Day Master): same element → Friend 比肩 / Rival 劫財; form produces world → Joy 食神 / Speak-up 傷官; form controls world → Opportunity 偏財 / Steady money 正財; world controls form → Pressure 七殺 / Recognition 正官; world produces form → Insight 偏印 / Support 正印 (same polarity first, opposite polarity second). `production/ten-mirrors.json` holds all 100 with the feeling computed.
- Order: TM001…TM100 alternate Sky/Season; each character cycles its five forms and the worlds shift by three each round, so every (form, world) pair appears exactly once and each run of ten reels has ten different worlds and ten different feelings.

## 2. Fixed visual language for the ten feelings (so viewers learn to read them)
| Feeling | What the world does | The character's answer |
|---|---|---|
| Friend 比肩 | Others like them appear; the world matches them | At ease, playing with equals |
| Rival 劫財 | The world takes or swallows what is theirs | Loses shape, then finds their own line inside it |
| Joy 食神 | The world responds to their touch and grows | Play, making things, delight |
| Speak-up 傷官 | The world drinks them dry / demands more | Pours out everything, then an outburst, then "enough" |
| Opportunity 偏財 | Treasure appears, something to gamble on | Playful risk; a windfall |
| Steady money 正財 | The world yields to patient work | Tends it, then harvests; quiet reward |
| Recognition 正官 | The world gives them a path, a channel, a role | Accepts the shape; stands taller |
| Pressure 七殺 | The world squeezes, chases, holds them down | Fights to stay themselves; breaks free, breathless |
| Support 正印 | The world feeds, shelters, carries them | Rests, is restored |
| Insight 偏印 | Mirrors, echoes, seeing what others don't | Quiet, odd, alone, understanding |
