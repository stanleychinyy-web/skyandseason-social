# The Ten Mirrors — block one: ten reels (V1, 11 Nov 2026)

Approved by Stanley on 11 Nov 2026 ("ok schedule it for production") after he reviewed the draft "Ten-Mirrors-First-Ten-Draft.pdf". Base concept: `production/TEN_MIRRORS_PLAN.md` (his, 7 Nov). This file overrides that plan's sections 3, 4 and 6 for block one. Tracker: `production/ten-mirrors.json` (reels with `"block": 1`).

## 0. What Stanley asked for (binding)
- He likes the Ten Mirrors / Ten Gods concept but the story must be **relatable to normal people**: "does it need to be so directly showcase of the element? … can it be a concept or an emotion of a situation that can refer to this feeling so it can relate to more people?" So: **situation first.** Each reel is an everyday human moment that carries the feeling. The element is the costume, the world is the set; neither is the subject.
- 10 forms × 10 feelings = 100 reels, planned and judged **ten at a time**.
- Standing character-reel rules still apply (Earth Discovery plan §0a, LEARNINGS.md, PROMPT_GUARDS.md): every story shot is a real Veo animation; on-model in every frame; one character per reel; the form is fixed for the whole reel (no form change); no words on screen, no title cards, no episode numbers; bright, never dark; music only; quality over speed (regenerate a failed clip; let the post date slip and tell Stanley rather than queue a flawed reel); each reel goes to the review page and posts only after his **Approve reel**.

## 1. The test (before any video credits are spent)
Look at K1 and K2 side by side with no caption: can a stranger name the feeling in one or two words (late! / picked / jealous / playing / relief…)? If not, redraw the stills until they can. The first two seconds of V1 must already show the situation.

## 4. Shot plans
Each still prompt = the scene below + the form's reference sheet + the world look + the matching guard block from PROMPT_GUARDS.md, vertical 9:16, bright high-key light, no text. Each clip prompt = the motion line below + the guard lines; one paragraph (Enter sends).

### 1 · TM064 · The Closing Gate — Pressure — "barely making it in time"
- **K1:** inside a wide cave tunnel looking towards the mouth: two huge iron doors already half closed, a tall strip of bright daylight and green grass beyond; Season in his Jia Wood form mid-tunnel in three-quarter view facing the light, eyes wide, leaning forward, lit by the shaft of daylight.
- **V1:** the iron doors grind slowly closer and the strip of light narrows; he hurries forward upright along the path towards it with quick small steps, glancing up at the doors; dust motes in the light; the camera travels with him. No leap, no fall.
- **K2:** outside, sunny grass, the iron doors shut behind him; Season standing upright, one hand on his chest, breathless happy relief; one green leaf in the air just above his nose.
- **V2:** his shoulders drop as he breathes out; he looks back at the closed doors, then up; the leaf drifts down onto his nose; he laughs.
- **Music:** quick light pizzicato, dropping to one warm chord at the cut. Ambience: stone rumble → birdsong.

### 2 · TM007 · Not the Only One — Friend — "finding out you're not the only one"
- **K1:** a wide sunny ploughed field with neat rows of small round soil mounds, each topped with a tiny two-leaf sprout (no faces, no creatures); Sky in her Ji Earth form hovering low, hands together, glancing sideways shyly.
- **V1:** she does a small happy side-to-side wiggle in the air, then stops and looks down, surprised: the sprout on the nearest mound wiggles the same way.
- **K2:** wider: Sky mid-air with arms out, delighted; the sprouts across the whole field leaning in rhythm.
- **V2:** the sprouts bob side to side in waves across the field; she bobs along joyfully in the middle; slow pull-back.
- **Music:** a shy single xylophone line that the whole band joins.

### 3 · TM050 · Wait, Me? — Recognition — "being picked"
- **K1:** a garden path at warm golden dusk lined with many glowing candles and paper lanterns (bright, not dark); one round golden lantern floating just above and in front of Season in his Geng Metal form; he looks up at it, one hand pointing at his own chest, surprised.
- **V1:** the lantern drifts along the row and stops above him; he looks left and right, then points at himself; the candle flames lean towards him.
- **K2:** Season holding the glowing lantern in both hands in front of his chest, standing tall, a small proud smile; the candle path ahead.
- **V2:** he walks slowly and carefully forward holding it steady; the candles brighten one after another as he passes.
- **Guards:** Season metal form lines (never "blade" or "sword"; no armour; face and body stay white).
- **Music:** soft celesta, then a gentle rising string line.

### 4 · TM093 · One Small Leaf — Rival — "comparing yourself"
- **K1:** sunlit forest floor among enormous tree trunks; Sky in her Yi Wood form hovering in front three-quarter view, holding up one small bright green leaf in both hands, beaming with pride.
- **V1:** above and beside her a giant branch unfurls many huge leaves and its shade slides over her; she looks up, then down at her leaf; her smile fades and she lowers it.
- **K2:** one warm sunbeam through a gap in the canopy falls on Sky; she holds the small leaf up into the beam and it glows; chin lifted, a quiet determined smile.
- **V2:** she raises the leaf into the sunbeam; it glows brighter green; a second tiny leaf opens beside it; she lifts her chin and smiles.
- **Music:** bright, a brief hush in the shade, then brighter than before.

### 5 · TM056 · For No Reason — Joy — "doing it just for fun"
- **K1:** a bright snowy mountain pass under blue sky; Season in his Bing Fire form (phoenix-feather ears and plume in red to gold, no flames) standing beside a small lopsided snow figure with two pebble eyes, both hands on it, concentrating happily.
- **V1:** he pats the snow figure; it softens and melts down; a cluster of colourful flowers pops up where it stood; he gasps, hands to his cheeks.
- **K2:** Season standing among several patches of bright flowers in the snow, arms spread wide, laughing.
- **V2:** he turns slowly with his arms out; more flower patches bloom from the snow around him; he laughs. Nobody else is there.
- **Music:** playful guitar and whistle.

### 7 · TM068 · Now or Never — Opportunity — "taking the chance"
- **K1:** a bright turquoise sea in sunshine; Season in his Wu Earth form standing upright on a small flat rock; a glittering golden shell riding the crest of a small wave just out of reach; he stretches one hand towards it, up on his toes, eyes sparkling.
- **V1:** the wave carries the shell past; he stretches and misses; he looks down at his rock, then at the shell circling back on the next swell; he bounces lightly on his toes, deciding.
- **K2:** Season standing upright on the crest of a gentle wave, holding the golden shell high in one hand towards the sun, whooping; sparkling spray; the rock behind him.
- **V2:** he rides the gentle wave forward standing upright, holding the shell up to the sun, laughing.
- **Music:** a held breath, then a big bright release at the cut.

### 8 · TM095 · Just Watching — Insight — "the quiet one who notices"
- **K1:** a bright dawn garden of green sprouts with pink petals and floating seeds whirling busily through the air; Sky in her Ding Fire form hovering apart at the edge in front three-quarter view, chin resting on her hands, calmly watching.
- **V1:** petals and seeds whirl past her; she watches calmly; her eyes follow down to a single clear water droplet resting under a leaf; she tilts her head.
- **K2:** closer: Sky hovering low, holding one clear water droplet in both hands in front of her chest; her soft glow lights it; a soft rainbow arcs over the sprouts.
- **V2:** the droplet lights up from inside and a soft rainbow spreads across the garden; the petals slow and drift; she smiles to herself.
- **Guards:** fire-form lines (small softly curled flame tips, no tall flames, no rings); the droplet stays one clear water droplet.
- **Music:** busy and bright, thinning to one clear celesta note.

### 9 · TM002 · Someone Left the Light On — Support — "being looked after"
- **K1:** a field of tall clear crystals at golden hour; Season in his Ren Water form walking slowly with drooping shoulders and tired eyes; ahead, one crystal glows warm amber like a lit window beside a small mossy hollow with a pool of clear water.
- **V1:** he walks slowly towards the warm glow with his head low; he reaches the hollow and lifts his head; his eyes widen softly, touched.
- **K2:** Season standing in the mossy hollow leaning his back against the warm glowing crystal, eyes closed, a small smile, his colours brighter.
- **V2:** he leans back against the warm crystal; his water plume deepens from pale to rich blue; his eyes close; a small smile; the glow softens.
- **Music:** slow harp and soft strings; warm, not sad.

### 10 · TM049 · Said It — Speak-up — "finally saying it"
- **K1:** a bright calm sea with one smooth rock; Sky in her Xin Metal form hovering just above it in front three-quarter view, cheeks puffed, eyes squeezed shut, hands clenched at her sides, glowing softly.
- **V1:** she puffs up and holds it in, shakes her head, puffs up again; her glow builds each time; the sea goes still.
- **K2:** Sky with her arms flung wide and mouth open, a brilliant burst of white-gold light spreading from her across the water; a few gulls far off.
- **V2:** the light rolls out across the sea and fades; she brings both hands to her mouth, eyes wide; she lowers them, gives one small satisfied nod and drifts gently upward.
- **Guards:** metal/crystal lines (nothing cracks, peels or breaks; no shards; nothing grows spikes).
- **Music:** a rising hum, one bright chime at the burst, a beat of silence, a light closing phrase.
