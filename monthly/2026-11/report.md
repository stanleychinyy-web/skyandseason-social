# The Month Ahead with Sky & Season: November 2026

## Monthly energy overview

The BaZi month is 己亥, which in plain words is Yin Earth sitting over the Pig. Yin Earth is the sign we call The Fertile Field: soft, patient soil that is good at looking after things. The Pig is a Water animal, and its season on our calendar is The Bare Ground: frost on the surface, nothing showing above the soil, and a great deal going on underneath.

Put the two together and you get a picture of patient soil holding deep, quiet water. In traditional reading, Water is linked to wealth and flow, and Earth is what stores and contains. Earth keeps Water in check this month, so the mood is less about splashing and more about saving, shaping and keeping. Winter, by tradition, is the season of storing and rest.

That lands neatly on the Singapore calendar: 11.11 sales, bonus season, year-end gatherings and Christmas planning all at once. The sky's suggestion is simple: enjoy them, but budget first and browse second.

The catch, because there is always a catch, is worry. The Fertile Field is nurturing and practical, and its known weakness is worrying too much. Frost hides growth; it does not stop it. If you cannot see results yet, that is the season doing its job, not a sign that something is wrong.

## The month pillar 己亥

### The Heavenly Stem: Yin Earth, The Fertile Field

The top half of the month pillar is Yin Earth. Think of garden soil rather than a mountain: nurturing, practical, patient. It is good for caring for people, cooking, budgeting and generally keeping the household running. It is less good at letting things go, which is where the worrying comes from.

### The Earthly Branch: the Pig, deep Water

The bottom half is the Pig, a Water branch and the first of the three winter months. Our season name for it is The Bare Ground, and the landscape is bare ground under frost, nothing visible above the soil. That is storage, not emptiness.

### Hidden stems

Every branch carries hidden stems, and the Pig carries two. Yang Water, The Ocean, makes up about 70 percent of the branch, which is why the Pig reads as deep, moving water. Yang Wood, The Great Tree, makes up the remaining 30 percent: the seed of spring already tucked inside winter. So the month has a quiet second layer of new ideas under all that water. The pillar's polarity is Yin, so it works on you gently rather than loudly.

## Solar-term dates: Gregorian month versus BaZi month

Beginners often trip here, so we will spell it out. The Gregorian month runs 1 to 30 November. The BaZi month starts and ends on solar terms instead.

- The month 己亥 begins at Li Dong (Start of Winter) on Saturday 7 November 2026 at 5:52 PM SGT.
- The mid-month term is Xiao Xue (Minor Snow) on Sunday 22 November 2026 at 3:23 PM SGT. The pillar does not change here; it simply marks the deepening of the season.
- The month ends at Da Xue (Major Snow) on Monday 7 December 2026 at 10:52 AM SGT, when the next pillar takes over.

So 1 to 7 November, up to 5:52 PM, still belong to the previous month 戊戌. If your first week of November feels like October, in BaZi terms it is. All times are Singapore time.

## Five Element interactions

### Month stem versus month branch

Yin Earth over the Pig: Earth keeps Water in check. Earth controls Water in the five-element cycle, the way a riverbank shapes a river. Soft Yin Earth is a gentle check, not a dam: Water still flows, just with a shape. Money, time and energy go better this month with a container: a budget, a timetable, a list.

### Month versus the year 丙午

The year 2026 is 丙午, Yang Fire over the Horse. Two things happen when the month meets the year.

First, the year's Yang Fire feeds the month's Yin Earth. Fire produces Earth in the element cycle, so the year keeps warming the month's patient soil. The Fertile Field has the year's sun on its back, which is one reason this winter month feels steadier than it might.

Second, the month's Water branch keeps the year's Fire branch in check. The Pig's deep Water cools the Horse's Fire. A hot, fast-moving year gets a calmer month: a chance to slow down, bank what you have gained and let the pace settle before December.

The year gives the month warmth; the month gives the year a breather. For most people that reads as a grounded November, provided you work at the soil's speed rather than the Horse's.

## Career and wealth

Water is traditionally the element of wealth and flow, and this month Water is contained by Earth. In plain terms: money is present, but the month favours those who shape it rather than those who chase it. Steady income, savings plans, year-end budgets and careful bonus decisions sit well with the energy. Big impulsive moves sit less well.

At work, Yin Earth rewards the quiet, dependable contributor. Finishing what you started, tidying up the year's loose ends, writing the plan for 2027 and looking after your team all fit. Hoping for a dramatic announcement? Remember the frost: results may be forming underground and show in a later month.

The hidden Yang Wood inside the Pig is the 30 percent already thinking about spring. Keep a notebook: November's ideas may not be ready to launch, but they are worth planting.

And for the 11.11 sales: the sky is not telling you to skip them. It is telling you to write the list before you open the app.

## Relationships and personal development

The Fertile Field is the nurturer of the ten signs, so this is a natural month for looking after people: cooking for family, hosting the first of the year-end gatherings, checking in on friends who have gone quiet. Small, consistent care tends to land better than grand gestures. The Water underneath keeps feelings flowing, so conversations can go deeper if you let them.

The catch, again, is worry. Yin Earth can fuss. If you find yourself replaying conversations or over-managing other people's problems, that is the month's known weak spot talking. Decide once, then let the soil rest.

For personal growth, winter is by tradition the season of storing and rest. That is permission, not laziness. Sleep, reflection, finishing books and quiet planning all count. The Bare Ground looks idle; underneath, roots are doing their best work of the year.

## The ten Day Masters

Your Day Master is the element of the day you were born; it decides which of the ten forces the month brings. The first force below comes from the month stem, Yin Earth; the second from the Pig.

**Yang Wood, The Great Tree.** The month brings The Steward, steady money. Earth is what Wood takes from, so this is a month for income you can count on and careful spending. The branch adds The Mystic, intuition: trust the quiet hunch about where to put your resources.

**Yin Wood, The Climbing Vine.** The month brings The Visionary, lucky breaks. Opportunities tend to show up sideways, through people and chance. The branch adds The Sage, help and learning, so mentors and good advice are close by. Say yes to the coffee invitation.

**Yang Fire, The Sun.** The month brings The Rebel, speaking up. You will want to say what you think, and the month gives you the platform. The branch adds The Crucible, pressure, so pick your moments and your tone. Honest, warm and brief works; blunt and tired does not.

**Yin Fire, The Candle Flame.** The month brings The Muse, ideas and fun. Creativity, hobbies and good company come easily. The branch adds The Guardian, recognition: the work you share may get noticed. A nice month to show what you have been making.

**Yang Earth, The Mountain.** The month brings The Shadow, competition. Yin Earth is your same element with the opposite polarity, so you meet peers, rivals and comparisons. The branch adds The Visionary, lucky breaks. Treat the rivalry as a pace-setter and let the luck find you while you work.

**Yin Earth, The Fertile Field.** The month brings The Sovereign, confidence. This is your own element, so you feel more like yourself and more sure of your footing. The branch adds The Steward, steady money. A solid month for building, saving and taking the lead quietly.

**Yang Metal, The Raw Blade.** The month brings The Sage, help and learning. Earth produces Metal, so the month feeds you: support from elders, teachers and institutions. The branch adds The Muse, ideas and fun. Learn something new and enjoy it.

**Yin Metal, The Jewel.** The month brings The Mystic, intuition. Your instincts are sharper than your spreadsheets this month. The branch adds The Rebel, speaking up, so you may surprise people with a well-timed honest word. Say it kindly.

**Yang Water, The Ocean.** The month brings The Guardian, recognition. Earth controls Water, which in traditional reading means structure, responsibility and being seen. The branch adds The Sovereign, confidence, because the Pig is your own element. Lead, and accept the credit.

**Yin Water, The Rain and Mist.** The month brings The Crucible, pressure. Earth leans on Water, so deadlines and expectations feel heavier. The branch adds The Shadow, competition. Pace yourself, ask for help early and remember the pressure is seasonal. The month that squeezes also sharpens.

## Practical monthly guidance

- Write the 11.11 list before you open the app, and set the budget before bonus season sets it for you.
- Use the first week, which still belongs to the previous month, to finish October's loose ends; start new plans after 7 November, 5:52 PM.
- Put one container around your money this month: a savings transfer, a simple spreadsheet or an envelope. Earth shaping Water is the whole point.
- Host small. The Fertile Field loves feeding people, and year-end gatherings go better in a kitchen than a ballroom.
- Notice when you are worrying on a loop. Make the decision once, write it down and let the soil rest.
- Keep a notebook for spring ideas. The hidden Yang Wood in the Pig means good seeds arrive early and disguised.
- Snakes, Monkeys and Yin Water Day Masters: build extra buffer into November. Everyone else: rest is on the plan, not off it.

All of the above is traditional interpretation, not a prediction of events; how November actually meets you depends on your full birth chart, which is what a personal reading at skyandseason.com looks at.
