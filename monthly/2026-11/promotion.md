# Paid promotion notes: The Month Ahead, November 2026

Nothing is boosted and no budget is set until Stanley approves it.

## Objective
Reach people in Singapore who have never heard of BaZi, hold them through the 60-second reel, and send them to the free monthly reading on skyandseason.com. Secondary: grow @theskyandseason with followers who like the plain-English style.

## Suggested audience
Singapore first (Malaysia as a secondary test), ages 25 to 45, interests in personal finance, budgeting, self-improvement, astrology, journaling and planning. Exclude existing followers for the cold test. Retarget people who watched 50 percent or more of the reel with the conversion story.

## Why this hook works
It opens with a wallet, not a Chinese term, so a stranger has nothing to decode in the first three seconds. It lands in 11.11 and bonus season, when money is already on everyone's mind, and it promises one small, concrete idea (a saving month) rather than fate or fortune. The rest of the reel then earns the brand words one at a time: element, Sky, Season, your element.

## Test hook alternatives
- 11.11 is coming. So is a saving month.
- This November, growth is underground.

## Notes
Keep the ad general; no personal predictions in ad copy. Avoid hype words and promises of luck in the primary text. Use the reel caption's first two paragraphs as the ad primary text. Run the hook variants against each other for three days before scaling, and judge on 3-second hold rate and link taps, not likes. The reel names no Chinese terms on screen apart from the month pillar in the caption, so it is safe for a cold audience.
