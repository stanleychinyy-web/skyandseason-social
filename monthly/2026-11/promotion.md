# Paid promotion notes: The Month Ahead, November 2026

Nothing is boosted and no budget is set until Stanley approves it.

## Objective
Reach people in Singapore who have never heard of BaZi and get them to watch the reel through, then tap to the free monthly reading on skyandseason.com.

## Suggested audience
Singapore (and Malaysia as a secondary test), 25–45, interests in personal finance, budgeting, self-improvement, astrology, journaling and planning; exclude existing followers for the cold test, retarget reel viewers (50%+) with the conversion story.

## Why this hook works
It opens with a wallet, not a Chinese term, so a stranger has nothing to decode. It lands during 11.11 and bonus season, when money is already on everyone's mind, and it promises a small, concrete idea (a saving month) rather than fate or fortune.

## Test hook alternatives
- 11.11 is coming. So is a saving month.
- November's secret: the growth is underground.

## Notes
Keep the ad general; no personal predictions in ad copy. Avoid words like guarantee or luck in the primary text. Use the reel caption's first two sentences as the ad primary text. Run the hook variants against each other for three days before scaling.
