# Paid promotion notes: The Month Ahead, December 2026

Nothing is boosted and no budget is set until Stanley approves it.

## Objective
Reach people in Singapore who have never heard of BaZi, hold them through the 60-second reel, and send them to the free monthly reading and the 2027 year mark on skyandseason.com. Secondary: grow @theskyandseason with followers who like the plain-English style.

## Suggested audience
Singapore first (Malaysia as a secondary test), ages 25 to 45, interests in productivity, decluttering, personal finance, self-improvement, astrology and journaling. Exclude existing followers for the cold test. Retarget people who watched 50 percent or more of the reel with the conversion story and the 2027 mark line.

## Why this hook works
It opens on something everyone in December is already thinking about, resolutions, and flips it: skip them, make one decision. There is nothing to decode in the first three seconds and no Chinese term. It promises one small, concrete idea rather than fortune, and the rest of the reel earns the brand words one at a time: element, Sky, Season, your element. The year-mark line gives a second, dated reason to visit the site before February.

## Test hook alternatives
- One clean decision beats ten resolutions.
- December is the clearing-out month.

## Notes
Keep the ad general; no personal predictions in ad copy. Avoid hype words and promises of luck in the primary text. Use the reel caption's first two paragraphs as the ad primary text, so the ad uses the site's Inner names (The Arbiter's Blade over The Waiting Seed) and nothing else. Run the hook variants against each other for three days before scaling, and judge on 3-second hold rate and link taps, not likes. The reel, the stories, the caption and the ad all use the Inner lens only: no animals, no stem names, no Chinese characters anywhere a cold audience sees them. The classic names appear only in the last section of the long report. No boost or budget without Stanley's explicit OK.

## Naming lens
The ad, like the reel and stories, uses the site's Inner names for both halves of the month: The Arbiter's Blade (the month's element, Yang Metal) over The Waiting Seed (the month's season, a Water season). No animals, stem names or Chinese characters in anything a cold audience sees.

## Site link to feature
For your own 2027 mark: free chart at skyandseason.com → Calendar → February 2027. (The caption and story 3 carry this line.)
